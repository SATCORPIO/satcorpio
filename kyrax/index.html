Cognitive core

The mind behind the system.

Five capabilities that everything else on this page is built from — and the five clusters the core has just condensed into.

01

AI reasoning systems

Structured reasoning over live system state, not single-shot prompts — the layer that decides what a request actually means before anything acts on it.

02

Data processing

Ingestion, normalisation and enrichment across every division's telemetry, so one question can be answered from many systems at once.

03

Pattern recognition

Behavioural and operational signal — what changed, what is anomalous, and what has happened before under the same conditions.

04

Decision support

Recommendations with the reasoning attached. Operators stay in the loop; the system argues its case rather than acting unannounced.

05

Learning frameworks

Feedback from real operations folded back into the models, so the system's judgement improves with the ecosystem instead of ageing against it.
