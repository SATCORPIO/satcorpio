What is NAMTAR

Every decision changes your future.

NAMTAR is a seamless open-world survival game built around a single idea. Explore an enormous planet, construct thriving settlements, uncover lost civilisations, research advanced technologies, transfer your consciousness, command robotic companions, and survive against both the planet itself and everything that came before you.

The world is not a backdrop. Namtar is a rocky oceanic super-Earth — 1.3× Earth across, 1.2 G at the surface, 58% ocean, and geologically still very much awake. The same survey data that draws the planet in orbital view draws the ground you land on.

The game drops you underneath all of it. You make landfall on a world that has been mapped from orbit and walked by almost no one — the volcanic fracture that glows through the night side, the closed basin holding the densest life signal on the planet, and a desert continent with no surface water at all. Survey it, survive it, and work out what the earlier surveys missed.

Living planet

The world runs whether you are there or not.

  • Dynamic weather and seasons
  • Full day / night cycle
  • Ocean ecosystems and deep oceans built for submarine gameplay
  • Wildlife behaviour rather than wildlife spawns
  • Environmental hazards and realistic terrain
  • A massive atmosphere with vertical airspace — air combat and naval warfare

Research & progression

No levels. Discovery.

Progression is not a bar that fills. It is what you find and manage to understand.

  • Ancient technology
  • Blueprints and schematics
  • Alien artifacts
  • Long-running research projects

Every discovery expands what is possible.

Consciousness system

Death is not always the end.

Transfer your consciousness into new cloned bodies or synchronised robotic frames — your journey continues, and an entirely different set of gameplay possibilities opens with it.
