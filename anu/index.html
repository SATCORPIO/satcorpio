The concierge difference

A methodical pipeline, not a proposal.

Every engagement runs the same route from first conversation to deployment, so nothing is discovered late and nothing is scoped twice.

Discovery

A structured questionnaire that establishes what the work is, what it is not, and what it will cost.

Scope

A signed statement of work: fixed deliverables, phase boundaries, and a number attached to each.

Execution

Build against the SOW, reported weekly. Scope changes go through the same gate as the original scope.

Deployment

Handoff, documentation, and the thing actually running in production under your control.
